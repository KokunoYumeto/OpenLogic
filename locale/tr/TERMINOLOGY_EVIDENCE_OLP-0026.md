# Turkish Functions terminology — OLP-0020--0026

The edition uses `tanım kümesi / değer kümesi / görüntü kümesi` for
domain/codomain/range; `bire bir / örten / bire bir örten`; `sol ters / sağ
ters` with their equations; `bileşke`; `kısmi fonksiyon / tümel fonksiyon`;
and `seri bağıntı` with its quantified definition.

Evidence includes Turkish Mathematical Society terminology, Ankara University
course notes, the 2013 MEB curriculum, and Turkish university logic sources.
The evidence record is not an institutional endorsement.

`tam fonksiyon` is deliberately avoided here because it collides with the
complex-analysis term “entire function.”
