# Turkish terminology evidence — OLP-0027--OLP-0040

Date: 2026-08-13

Scope: the complete reader-reachable `size-of-sets` chapter at official Open
Logic commit `9620cc73f9c8e0ad003c514a5d3748f29611c4c0`.

## Evidence-backed reader terms

- `sayılabilir`, `sayılamaz`, `sayılabilir sonsuz`, and `eş güçlü` follow the
  terminology used in Kırıkkale University's Turkish *Soyut Matematik* notes,
  Chapter 9: <https://eders.kku.edu.tr/pluginfile.php/450/mod_resource/content/1/sm-dersnot%20H%C3%BCseyinBilgi%C3%A7.pdf>.
- `kardinalite`, `sayılabilir`, `sayılamaz`, and `Schröder-Bernstein Teoremi`
  are independently attested in Çankırı Karatekin University's Turkish
  mathematics course description:
  <https://krtknadmn.karatekin.edu.tr/files/matematik/2023-2024%20Bahar%20Ders%20izlencesi%28TR%29.pdf>.
- The local Turkish mathematical witness shelf directly attests
  `Cantor'un köşegen yöntemi`; `köşegenleştirme` is the corresponding process
  noun used in this edition.
- TÜBA Open Course Ware logic notes attest `ikileme fonksiyonu` for a function
  that encodes an ordered pair as one set or number; the definition remains
  explicit at first use:
  <https://acikders.tuba.gov.tr/pluginfile.php/1650/mod_resource/content/1/unite9.pdf>.

The edition therefore uses `numaralandırma`, `sayılabilir` / `sayılamaz`,
`eş güçlü` / `eş güçlülük`, `kardinalite`, `Cantor'un köşegen yöntemi`, and
`indirgeme`. `Denk` is not alternated with `eş güçlü`, because this locale
already reserves `denklik` for equivalence relations.

## Collision and adverse decisions

- `pairing function` is `ikileme fonksiyonu` and is defined at first use. Bare
  `eşleme fonksiyonu` is rejected because `eşleme` is the established generic
  term for map/mapping; `çiftleme fonksiyonu` is rejected because it readily
  denotes doubling, $f(x)=2x$.
- `cofinite` is rendered descriptively as `tümleyeni sonlu`; no unsupported
  standalone coinage is promoted.
- English `enumerable` and `countable` share the Turkish reader surface
  `sayılabilir` in this chapter. The exact English source token remains bound
  in the closure ledger, so this does not conflate source provenance.

These are active producer terms for publication and continuation. They do not
claim community certification and do not impose a separate review gate.
