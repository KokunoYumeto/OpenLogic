## Required distinctions

- Formal uses keep `Dedekind-sonsuz` distinct from ordinary `sonsuz`. ZF does
  not prove that every ordinary infinite set is Dedekind-infinite; the
  chapter does not silently import a choice principle.
- `Dedekind cebiri` is a successor structure, whereas `Dedekind kesimi` is
  the earlier real-number construction.
- `$f$ altındaki kapanış` is closure under function iteration, not the
  relation-theoretic `geçişli kapanış`.
- `izomorf` preserves structure; `eş güçlü` asserts only a bijection.
- Open Logic writes `\comp{f}{g}=g\circ f`; Turkish prose preserves that
  composition order in the Schröder-Bernstein proof.

The displayed Dedekind-algebra construction, induction result, and
Schröder-Bernstein proof require no Axiom of Choice once their sets and
functions exist.
